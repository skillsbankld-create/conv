🏆 How the Game Works
Each scenario shows a real workplace conflict. Read it carefully.
Choose from 3 responses: Constructive, Fight, or Avoidance.
Best choice = +2 points + Safety up. Wrong choice = points and safety drop.
If time runs out, the avoidance option is automatically selected.
At the end, receive your Dialogue Level: Master, Skilled, or Still Learning.
💡 The Goal
→The goal is NOT to win arguments — it is to keep dialogue open.
→Great communicators create safety, listen deeply, and speak honestly.
→This simulation is based on the Crucial Conversations framework by VitalSmarts.
